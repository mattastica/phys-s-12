Workflow Enhancement

We have been using scissors to cut the double sided tape which makes inaccurate cuts, muddies the scissors, and often leaves the tape uneven which can result in a non-level surface when milling. The latter can ruin your traces. Solution: a razor has been magnetized to the side of the drawers near the PCB mill. Just pull a piece of double sided tape to length, tape the edge on the table, and cut it with the razor. It's 3 small slices and you have a perfectly clean cut. I showed this workflow to Victor.

CNC Enclosure

Water Resistant Case

Gurmani and I decided to team up to use the rotary encoder as a waterproof remote control for some future device we'll do in the networking project. She designed an enclosure for the mill and the idea was to vacuum seal a plastic mold for it.

Process

Mill multiple thin MDF layers separately, laminate them together to reach full height. No endmill depth problem, no structural risk. Sand smooth, chamfer edges lightly with a chisel, vacuum form PETG over the positive. Done.

She documented this in great detail.
